About the index

Uncured rubber scrap has no public price. Buyers and sellers sign multi-year agreements with no fair way to adjust them as markets move. This index gives them one.

Publisher

The index is published at market-recycle.com. Questions, comments on the methodology and requests for data go to index@market-recycle.com.

Independence

The index is built so that no one's judgment, including the publisher's, enters any published value:

  • Every input is public data from the U.S. Census Bureau, the World Bank, the Federal Reserve and the Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye.
  • No company's transaction prices are used to calculate any value.
  • The formula and weights are fixed for each methodology version and published in full.
  • The code, inputs and published values are kept in a public repository with a dated history of every change, so any value can be recomputed independently.

Complaints

If you believe a published value was calculated incorrectly, or that the methodology was not followed, email index@market-recycle.com with the month and the reason. You will get an acknowledgement within five business days and a written answer within 30 days. If a calculation error is confirmed, a correction notice is added to the history beside the affected value.

Annual review

Once a year the publisher reviews whether the methodology still measures what it is meant to, and publishes the result. Any change that follows goes through the 30-day notice process described in the methodology.

Using the index

Anyone may cite the index and its published values, with attribution to market-recycle.com. Parties who refer to it in an agreement should define what happens if a monthly value is not published. The index is not investment, legal or purchasing advice, and it is provided without warranty.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and collects no personal information. Emails you send are used only to answer you.

The index measures price movement. It is not a price quote, an offer, or investment advice.
