What goes into it

The index moves with four public inputs. Each is measured against its level in the base month.

InputWhat it measuresSourceWeight
Scrap export valueValue per kilogram of U.S. exports of rubber waste and scrap, HTS 4004.00.00, three-month averageU.S. Census Bureau
Virgin rubberTSR20 and RSS3 natural rubber prices; U.S. import value per kilogram of SBR and polybutadieneWorld Bank; U.S. Census Bureau
Buyer currenciesIndian rupee, Turkish lira, Brazilian real and Thai baht against the dollar, weighted by each country's share of U.S. scrap exportsFederal Reserve; Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye
EnergyBrent and WTI crude oil, monthly averageWorld Bank

Weights are set by the methodology in effect for each published value. Read the methodology.

Check it yourself

The code, the downloaded inputs and every published value are kept in a public repository with a dated history of every change. Download the full history as CSV, or follow the steps to reproduce a value.
